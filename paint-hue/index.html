SAFETY ZONE

Parents' Corner & FAQs

Answers about privacy, offline play and drawing devices.

📖 What is PaintHue?

PaintHue is a digital coloring app for Android with three modes: Tap-to-Fill guided coloring, a pencil Sketch brush, and a Free Draw blank canvas. It is made for anyone who wants a relaxing way to color.

🎨 What are the three modes in PaintHue?

Tap to Fill lets you color segmented vector outlines with a tap. Sketch Brush lets you color and shade by hand with an adjustable pencil, eraser and transparency. Free Draw gives you a blank canvas with the full color picker and brush controls.

🖼️ How many coloring templates does PaintHue have?

PaintHue includes over 200 black and white outline templates across themes such as nature and flowers, cute animals, prehistoric creatures and outer space.

👶 Is PaintHue safe for children?

PaintHue is rated 3+ on Google Play and is a single-player coloring game. It does not require an account, and the Google Play data safety section lists no data collected and no data shared with third parties.

🔒 Does PaintHue collect my personal data?

No. PaintHue does not require an account, and its privacy policy states that it does not collect, store or share personally identifiable information. Your progress is saved on your device. Read the PaintHue Privacy Policy.

✈️ Can I use PaintHue offline?

Yes, after setup. PaintHue downloads its level files and sound assets from the internet, and once they are downloaded you can color offline. Your saved progress stays on your device.

💸 Is PaintHue free?

PaintHue is free to download from Google Play.

✍️ Which drawing devices does PaintHue support?

PaintHue supports multi-touch input, capacitive stylus pens and Samsung S-Pen devices. Pressure sensitivity adapts to your input hardware.

💾 Can I save my finished artwork?

Yes. You can export your finished or in-progress artwork as a PNG image to your device gallery. PaintHue also saves your coloring progress on your device so you can come back later.

🎒 Are there coloring templates for educational themes?

Yes, the templates are sorted into themed groups such as animals, nature and space. This helps children recognize vocabulary terms and nature structures as they play.

📲 Where can I download PaintHue?

PaintHue is available for Android on Google Play. Search for PaintHue on Google Play or use the download link on this page.
