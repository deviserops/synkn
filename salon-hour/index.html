SalonHour FAQ

Quick answers about plans, booking pages and the web portal.

What is SalonHour?

SalonHour: Salon Management is an Android app that helps salon owners and beauty businesses manage specialists, services, packages, customers and appointments digitally instead of on paper.

Who is SalonHour for?

SalonHour is built for business users such as salon owners and service providers. Customers do not need the app: each business gets its own booking page link that customers open in a web browser to book directly.

What is the SalonHour booking page?

Every business owner gets a booking page URL for direct booking. It shows your business details and location, and you can share it on social media or send it to customers.

Is SalonHour free?

Yes. The free plan includes 20 appointments per month, up to 10 services and 1 package, 1 specialist (solo owner), your booking page, a basic booking confirmation reminder and unlimited customer contact management. Premium is a paid subscription in the app.

What does SalonHour Premium include?

Premium includes unlimited appointments, unlimited services and packages, unlimited specialists and staff management, your booking page, automated reminders (instant, 24 hours and 1 hour before), unlimited customer contact management, priority support and business analytics.

What can a salon owner manage in SalonHour?

Owners can manage specialist timings and block timings, business hours, the business profile, customers, appointments, services and packages, and notification settings. The dashboard shows monthly revenue and completed appointments.

Is there a web portal for my front desk?

Yes. Business owners can log in to the SalonHour web portal from a computer or tablet. It shows a dashboard, upcoming appointments, customers, services and packages, and a way to book new appointments through your booking page. Services and packages are view-only there, and the owner edits them in the app.

Where is SalonHour available?

SalonHour is available on Google Play in Australia, Canada, India, Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, the United Kingdom and the United States.

Is SalonHour available on iPhone?

SalonHour is currently available for Android on Google Play. Customers can still book through a business's booking page on any device with a web browser.

How does SalonHour handle my data?

SalonHour stores your business and appointment data so the service can run. The Google Play data safety section lists the data types the app may collect or share, says data is encrypted in transit, and says you can request that your data be deleted. Read the SalonHour Privacy Policy.

How do I sign up for SalonHour?

Download SalonHour from Google Play and sign up or log in with your email or Google account.
