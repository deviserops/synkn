SlabStack FAQ

Quick answers about the game, offline play and privacy.

What is SlabStack?

SlabStack is a free one-tap isometric stacking game for Android. You drop moving slabs onto a tower, and any overhang is sliced off, so precise timing keeps your tower wide and tall.

Does SlabStack work offline?

Yes. SlabStack is fully offline. You do not need Wi-Fi or mobile data, and there are no extra downloads after install.

What game modes does SlabStack have?

Three shape modes: Classic Rectangle, Conical Cylinder and Prism Triangle. Triangle is the hardest because misplaced slabs lose the most surface.

Does SlabStack collect my data?

No. The Google Play data safety section lists no data collected and no data shared with third parties. Read the SlabStack Privacy Policy.

What age is SlabStack suitable for?

SlabStack is rated 3+ on Google Play.

Which devices can run SlabStack?

SlabStack is available for Android on Google Play.
