The Big Idea

Swimming Is Something You Earn

Every level starts with no swimming. Explore, find the red SWIM switch, then go back for the stars under the water.

1

Wade and Double Jump

Run, jump and double jump across gaps. In water you wade slowly and can only hop from the floor, so deep pools are out of reach at first. Steps at each end mean you can never get stuck.

2

Find the SWIM Switch

A red button labelled SWIM is hidden somewhere further along the level. Step on it and swimming unlocks for the rest of that level.

3

Dive for the Stars

Float, dive and stroke through the pool to collect the star below the surface, then swim out and head for the exit door.

Field Guide to the Locals

Learn how each creature behaves and you'll know when to stomp, swim or run.

Crab

On the ground

Patrols back and forth. Stomp it from above to defeat it and bounce, but touch its side and you lose.

Fish

In the water

Wanders its pool, then hunts you in any direction when you swim close. It can't be defeated, so time your swim.

Bird

In the air

Glides over the level at a safe height for walking beneath. Land on its back to take it out.

Hawk

Later worlds

Shakes and shows a red warning, then dives where you were standing. Move and it misses, or stomp it as it comes down.

TideShift FAQ

Quick answers about swimming, levels and privacy.

What is TideShift?

TideShift is a free offline pixel-art platformer for Android. You run, double jump and wade through water, find a hidden SWIM switch to unlock swimming, then dive for stars that wading can't reach.

How does swimming work in TideShift?

Swimming is locked at the start of every level. Step on the red SWIM switch to unlock it for the rest of that level. Then you can float, dive and stroke through pools to reach stars under the surface and leave the water.

How many levels does TideShift have?

TideShift has 500 levels in 10 worlds, with 50 levels in each world. Levels get harder as you progress, and every level has 3 optional stars to collect.

What are the controls in TideShift?

TideShift is played in landscape with two-thumb touch controls: jump and dive on the left, and move left and right on the right. You can turn the touch controls off in Settings.

What enemies are in TideShift?

Crabs patrol the ground, fish hunt you underwater, and birds and hawks fly overhead. You can stomp crabs, birds and hawks from above. Fish cannot be defeated, so swim away from them.

Does TideShift work offline?

Yes. You can play TideShift without an internet connection, and your progress is saved on your device.

Is TideShift free?

TideShift is free to play. It has no ads at the moment, and this page and the privacy policy will be updated if ads are added.

Does TideShift collect my data?

No. TideShift does not require an account and does not collect personal data. Your stars, unlocked levels and settings are stored only on your device. Read the TideShift Privacy Policy.

Is TideShift available on iPhone?

TideShift is built for Android phones. An iPhone version has not been announced.

Where can I download TideShift?

Download TideShift from Google Play by searching for TideShift by Synkn, or use the download link on this page when it is available.
